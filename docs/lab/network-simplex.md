# The network simplex, pivot by pivot

The basis tree, the arcs at their bounds, the potentials and the reduced costs; the entering arc, the cycle, the step and the leaving arc; at the end the certificate. Choose the initial structure (the example's tree or Phase 0); ◀ ▶ one step at a time, «Run» to watch them all; the «Instance» menu, «Random», «Edit…» and «Balances…» change the network.
