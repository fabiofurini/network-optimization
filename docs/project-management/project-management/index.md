!!! nota "Prerequisites"

    - From [Chapter 1 «Graphs»](https://fabiofurini.github.io/network-optimization/graphs/graphs/): directed graphs, directed paths and cycles.

    - From [Chapter 3 «Shortest paths»](https://fabiofurini.github.io/network-optimization/trees-and-paths/shortest-paths/): the topological order of an acyclic graph, the algorithm for acyclic graphs and longest paths, potentials and reduced costs, the LP model of a shortest path and its dual.

    - From the module Linear Programming, [Chapter 9 «Duality theory»](https://fabiofurini.github.io/linear-programming/duality/09-duality/): the dual of an LP model and the weak duality theorem.

!!! chiave "Objectives"

    At the end of this chapter you will be able to:

    - describe a project by its activities, durations and precedences, and draw its project network with the START and FINISH nodes;

    - compute the earliest and the latest start and finish times with the forward and the backward pass, and prove that the minimum project duration is the length of a critical path;

    - compute the total and the free slacks, and find the critical activities and all the critical paths;

    - draw the Gantt chart of a schedule;

    - write the LP model of the project duration and its dual, a longest path model;

    - predict the effect of a change of one duration on the project duration.
