# Downloads

The lecture notes, the exercises and the slides in PDF, with the same numbering of definitions, theorems, examples and exercises as the website, and the Colab notebooks. Text and figures are released under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en), the code under the MIT licence.

## Lecture notes

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 1 · Graphs**

    ---

    Directed and undirected graphs and their language: computer representations, the node–arc incidence matrix, paths, cycles and connectivity, trees, breadth-first and depth-first search.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-1-graphs.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-1-graphs.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 2 · Trees and paths**

    ---

    Minimum spanning trees (Kruskal, Prim, the cut and cycle properties) and shortest paths (acyclic graphs, Bellman–Ford, Dijkstra), with potentials as a certificate of optimality.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-2-trees-and-paths.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-2-trees-and-paths.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 3 · Flows**

    ---

    Network flow models and total unimodularity; maximum flow and minimum cut; minimum cost flow with negative cycles, potentials and reduced costs; transportation, transshipment and assignment problems.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-3-flows.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-3-flows.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 4 · The network simplex**

    ---

    The network simplex: bases as spanning trees, the pivot in the uncapacitated and capacitated cases, degeneracy.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-4-network-simplex.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-4-network-simplex.pdf)

-   :material-book-open-variant: **Lecture notes, volume 5 · Project management**

    ---

    Project management with the critical path method: project networks, earliest and latest times, slacks and critical paths.

    [:octicons-download-24: lecture-notes-5-project-management.pdf](pdf/lecture-notes-5-project-management.pdf)
